Lumina Space

A transformative digital experience redefining how light and space interact within virtual environments. This project explores the boundaries of generative design and real-time rendering.

Client

Aura Technologies

Role

Lead Design & Development

Year

2024

Expertise

WebGL Generative UI Spatial Audio

The Challenge

Aura Technologies approached us to create a digital platform that could visually articulate their complex architectural lighting systems. Traditional 2D renderings were insufficient; they needed a solution that allowed architects to 'feel' the light.

Our approach involved abandoning static assets entirely. We built a custom WebGL pipeline that simulates ray-tracing in real-time, directly within the browser, offering an unprecedented level of fidelity and interaction.

"The studio didn't just build a tool; they created an experiential canvas that fundamentally changed how we sell our vision."

— Sarah Jenkins, CDO Aura Tech
