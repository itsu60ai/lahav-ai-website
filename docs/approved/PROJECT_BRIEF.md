# LAHAV AI - Project Brief

STATUS: Stage 1 complete pending approval

## What we are building
The production website for LAHAV AI, an AI and software agency serving Israeli SMBs. Built from zero.

## Why
To turn relevant visitors into qualified leads who book a Discovery call, by building trust and
showing a real ability to build AI systems and software for businesses.

## Who it is for
Owners, CEOs, operations and marketing managers at businesses of roughly 2-50 employees who feel
operational pain: manual processes, disorder, overload, poorly handled leads, or a need for a
custom system. Business-plain language, not technical.

## What it sells
Smart CRM systems, business automations, website development, app development, and AI-powered
content creation. No public pricing - price follows Discovery and scoping.

## How success is measured
Discovery calls booked. Secondary: WhatsApp conversations and lead form submissions.

## Ground rules
- Total truthfulness: no invented clients, results, or testimonials (R9)
- AI drafts, human approves, then publish (R10)
- Lowest practical recurring cost, ideally domain-only (R13)
- Hebrew RTL in V1; English later but never blocked (R6)
- Accessibility-first construction; legal obligation still to be verified (R17, NV-1)
- Admin/content editing is authenticated and access-controlled; public site needs no login (R20)
