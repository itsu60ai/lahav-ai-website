# Stitch Content Cleanup Rules

Stitch is **not** a factual content source. Its HTML and screenshots are visual/structural references. Any claim in Stitch that is not supported by the PRD, Addendum or Approved Copy must be removed, rewritten neutrally or flagged for verification.

## Global removals / verification

Remove or verify all of the following before public release:

- invented copyright years such as `© 2024`
- invented emails, phone numbers, addresses or legal entity details
- fake clients, client logos, testimonials, case studies or social proof
- fabricated metrics, percentages, results, ROI, revenue, hours saved or performance claims
- awards, certifications, years of experience, number of projects or implementation timelines
- unsupported platform logos, named integrations, APIs, frameworks, app-store claims or technical capabilities
- unsupported claims of real-time operation, guaranteed performance, SEO outcomes, conversion outcomes, growth, profitability or zero-error automation
- unsupported "team" claims if no verified team information is supplied
- unsupported support/maintenance/training promises
- urgency, scarcity, "free consultation", "no obligation" or similar sales claims not approved in the PRD

## Page-specific high-risk examples from Stitch

### Home
Treat all promotional claims beyond the PRD as drafts. Do not publish fake social proof. Founder/company area may remain a neutral placeholder until approved story/content exists.

### Services
Remove or verify examples such as smart lead scoring, real-time dashboards, sentiment analysis, automatic client responses, advanced e-commerce, native/hybrid app capability, image/video generation, named APIs/platforms and any numeric performance claims.

### CRM
Remove or verify claims such as "real-time", "360° profile", collecting automatically from all channels, exact performance measurement, guaranteed error reduction, easy/fast adoption, full team training and unsupported channel/platform connections.

### Business Automations
Any displayed counts/metrics are fictional. Generic examples (trigger/condition/action/reminder) may be retained as examples only, never as a promise that every project includes them. Generic system categories may be shown, but do not imply verified integrations.

### Website Development
Remove or verify conversion claims, advanced SEO outcomes, performance guarantees, hosting/cloud/support commitments, e-commerce capability and unverified data/CRM integrations.

### Application Development
Remove or verify "clean code", "stable environment", guaranteed error reduction, instant/fast search, data-driven decision outcomes, training/support and any specific platform/framework/API/native-app capability.

### AI Content Creation
Remove or verify permissions hierarchies, version-control promises, Prompt Engineering as a guaranteed deliverable, production-volume/speed claims, Time-to-Market claims, performance claims and social proof. Human review/approval before publishing remains mandatory.

### About
Use `APPROVED_COPY.md` as factual authority. Do not invent founder biography, credentials, previous companies, team members, years, client history or measurable impact.

### Blog
All Stitch article titles, thumbnails, categories, dates and metadata are placeholders only. If no real approved articles exist at launch, hide the public Blog entry points while keeping CMS capability.

### Article Detail
All Stitch article copy, date, read time, quotes, claims, diagrams and related articles are placeholders only. Production content must come from real approved articles.

### Contact / Discovery
Do not invent response times, hours, free/no-obligation claims, research steps, "our team" actions, scheduling providers, contact details or guarantees. Form fields should be finalized in implementation.

### Privacy
The Stitch legal text is a layout placeholder only. Do not publish it as legal advice or as LAHAV AI's final privacy policy. Final text depends on the actual production stack, forms, analytics, cookies, hosting and processors.

### 404
Keep it simple. No business claims are needed.
