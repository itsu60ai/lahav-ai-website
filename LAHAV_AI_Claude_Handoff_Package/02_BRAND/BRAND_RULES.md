# LAHAV AI Brand Rules for Website Implementation

## Canonical Identity

- Deep Navy: `#0B1530`
- Royal Blue: `#1D4ED8`
- Electric Blue: `#2997FF`
- Cool Gray: `#F2F4F7`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary gradient: `#0B1530 -> #1D4ED8 -> #2997FF`

## Typography - V1 Hebrew Website

- Hebrew headings: **Heebo**
- Hebrew body/UI: **Assistant**
- English V1 is not in scope. If isolated English interface/display text is needed, keep it subordinate and visually compatible; do not replace the Hebrew type system.

## Logo Rules

- Use the official assets in this folder only.
- Light background -> `LAHAV_AI_Primary.svg` or approved dark/color variant.
- Dark background -> `LAHAV_AI_White.svg`.
- Monogram may be used only where a compact mark is appropriate.
- Never recreate LAHAV AI as plain text in place of the logo.
- Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, shadow, distort or add effects to the logo.

## Visual Language

- Clean, premium, intelligent, precise, modern, business-oriented.
- Favor system maps, workflows, interface-inspired compositions, modular geometry and intentional whitespace.
- Do not use robots, AI brains, cyberpunk/matrix motifs, generic neon 3D blobs, fake dashboards, fake clients or decorative tech cliches.
- Brand graphics may feel advanced, but must remain legible and business-relevant.

## RTL

- Native RTL layout, not an LTR layout mirrored after the fact.
- Directional process/forward arrows follow RTL progression where applicable.
- Non-directional icons such as WhatsApp, phone, search and settings are not mirrored.
