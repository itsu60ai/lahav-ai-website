# Service Page Scope Addendum

## Scope Update

V1 now includes dedicated internal service pages for:

- Website Development
- Application Development
- AI Content Creation

CRM and Business Automations remain the two featured services, but all five core services now have dedicated detail pages.

This addendum supplements the main PRD and does not replace its brand, truth, navigation, conversion, accessibility or RTL rules.

## Global Rules for the Three Added Service Pages

- Use the approved LAHAV AI Brand Book and official logos as visual authority.
- Maintain one coherent LAHAV AI website system.
- Native Hebrew RTL. Heebo for Hebrew headings, Assistant for Hebrew body/UI.
- Business-first framing: understand the business need/process before choosing technology.
- End with Discovery as the primary CTA and WhatsApp as the secondary CTA.
- Do not invent clients, results, ROI, metrics, pricing, timelines, platforms, integrations, technical capabilities, awards, certifications or support promises.

## Website Development Service Detail

- Explain that a business website should support a clear business purpose and user journey, not exist only as a visual layer.
- Visually communicate structure, UX, content, forms/process entry points, responsive behavior and the relationship between the website and wider business processes.
- Show the global LAHAV AI delivery journey: Discovery, specification, planning, build, testing, delivery/onboarding.
- Generic system connections may be visualized, but do not name or imply unverified platforms, integrations, e-commerce capability, advanced SEO promises, hosting, maintenance or performance guarantees.

## Application Development Service Detail

- Explain custom application development as a response to a defined business need or process.
- Visually communicate user flows, screens, actions, information movement and structured interaction without inventing product features.
- Show the approved LAHAV AI delivery journey from Discovery and specification through build, testing and delivery/onboarding.
- Do not claim native iOS/Android, hybrid, app-store publishing, specific frameworks, APIs, real-time capabilities, authentication models or other technical features unless verified later.

## AI Content Creation Service Detail

- Explain AI-assisted business content creation as a structured workflow rather than autonomous publishing.
- Visually communicate: business objective -> content brief -> AI-assisted draft -> human review -> approval -> publish/use.
- Human review and approval are mandatory before public publishing of AI-generated content.
- Do not invent channels, content volumes, production speed, performance results, autonomous publishing, specific generation platforms or unsupported media capabilities.
