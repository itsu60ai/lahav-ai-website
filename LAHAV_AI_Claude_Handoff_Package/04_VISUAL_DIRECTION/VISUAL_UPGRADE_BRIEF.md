# Visual Upgrade Brief - Ultra Premium LAHAV AI

## Goal

Take the approved Stitch structures and turn them into an **ultra-premium, international-quality custom software / AI systems agency website**. The quality bar should feel closer to top product companies than to a generic agency template.

Working shorthand: **Apple-level restraint and polish + Stripe system storytelling + Linear precision + LAHAV AI brand + selective STG Hebrew/editorial energy.**

This is a quality benchmark, not a request to copy any site.

## Primary benchmark: Apple

Reference: https://www.apple.com/

Borrow the principles, not the look:
- exceptional whitespace and visual focus
- oversized, confident typography with clear hierarchy
- one primary idea per major section
- product-like, cinematic presentation
- high-quality transitions and scroll storytelling
- visual elements that feel intentional, not decorative
- very clear CTA hierarchy

Do **not** copy Apple's typography, product layouts, imagery, iconography, navigation, marketing copy or signature compositions.

## Stripe

Reference: https://stripe.com/

Use as inspiration for:
- layered system/product visuals
- connected infrastructure diagrams
- sophisticated gradients used with purpose
- interface fragments embedded into storytelling
- dense information that still feels ordered and premium
- strong transitions between light and dark visual zones

## Linear

Reference: https://linear.app/

Use as inspiration for:
- precision and restraint
- refined dark surfaces
- crisp interface details
- micro-interactions that feel fast and deliberate
- subtle depth and motion rather than decorative effects
- excellent spacing, typography and state design

## STG.AI

Reference: https://stgltd.com/ai/ and its linked AI pages.

Use selectively for:
- Hebrew-first editorial confidence
- technology/system visuals adapted to an Israeli audience
- more expressive section composition than a standard SaaS template
- mixed Hebrew/English brand moments where useful

Do not reuse STG's claims, clients, metrics, services, imagery or layouts.

## Vercel - secondary reference

Reference: https://vercel.com/

Use selectively for:
- clean technical atmosphere
- sharp grid logic
- motion around product/technical concepts
- minimal interaction chrome

## What to materially improve over Stitch

- Stitch currently gives a good structure but often looks like an AI/SaaS template. Reduce repetitive card grids.
- Increase editorial composition, asymmetry, scale contrast and intentional whitespace.
- Build stronger original LAHAV AI system maps, flow diagrams and interface-inspired visuals.
- Make hero sections more ownable and cinematic without using stock photography.
- Create stronger light/dark section rhythm.
- Treat important diagrams as hero assets, not tiny decorations.
- Use the LAHAV monogram/geometry subtly as a recurring motif, never as wallpaper spam.

## Motion direction

Allowed when useful:
- scroll-triggered reveals
- subtle sticky storytelling
- line/node animations in system diagrams
- interface panels entering with depth
- masked section transitions
- restrained parallax
- subtle gradient/light movement
- premium hover/focus states

Rules:
- no scroll hijacking
- no motion just to show off
- no heavy autoplay video dependency
- no cursor gimmicks
- no excessive glassmorphism/neon/glow
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- mobile motion should be simpler and cheaper
- animations must not block reading or CTA use

## Visual non-negotiables

- Keep LAHAV AI colors and official logo system.
- Keep Heebo + Assistant for V1 Hebrew.
- Native RTL.
- Keep Discovery primary and WhatsApp secondary.
- Keep all five service disciplines and current page scope.
- Do not make the entire site dark. Use controlled light/dark contrast.
- Do not sacrifice comprehension, accessibility or performance for spectacle.
