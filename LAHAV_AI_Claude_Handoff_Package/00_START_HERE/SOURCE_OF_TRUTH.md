# Source of Truth Hierarchy

When sources conflict, follow this order:

1. **Main PRD + Service Page Scope Addendum** - what must exist, what the site must communicate, V1 scope, truth rules.
2. **Approved Copy** - exact factual copy that has already been approved.
3. **Brand Rules + official logo assets** - brand identity, logo usage, colors and typography.
4. **Final Stitch pages** - approved structural/compositional baseline and design ideas. They are not a factual content source.
5. **Visual benchmark websites** - inspiration for polish and art direction only. Never copy branding/content/layouts verbatim.
6. **Claude recommendations** - architecture/implementation/design recommendations must be clearly identified as recommendations until approved.

## Critical change from the earlier Stitch phase

Stitch is **not the visual ceiling**. Claude Code may materially upgrade art direction, layout polish, motion, section transitions and system visuals, provided it preserves the approved business structure, page purpose, service scope, truth rules, CTA hierarchy and brand identity.
